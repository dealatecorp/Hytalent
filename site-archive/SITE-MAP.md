# HYtalent — complete public-site map & content archive

**Source:** https://hytalent.in/  
**Captured:** 12 October 2026  
**Coverage:** 38 public routes: all URLs in the site's published XML sitemap, plus the public Blog route exposed by its navigation. Every route below has a corresponding full-content Markdown capture in `pages/`. Image references remain as their original public URLs.

## How to use this archive

- **This file** is the information architecture and a concise content inventory for the redesign.
- **`pages/*.md`** is the source-of-truth content capture. It preserves page copy, headings, lists, linked images, testimonials, forms, and route-specific text.
- Repeated site chrome is intentionally excluded where it was not returned as main content; route-level registration/contact popups are retained when present, including their field labels and validation prompts.
- The original site publishes a duplicate/legacy ICC route. It is included separately to avoid losing content.

## Content inventory for redesign

The source is principally a corporate learning-and-development site. Preserve these content groups in the rebuild:

- Company narrative and trainer credentials
- 28 distinct service/program routes, including the legacy Mid-Career Refresher page
- Image gallery and client-logo gallery
- Homepage’s testimonials, feature cards, and signup calls-to-action
- Contact/register forms and their supplied fields
- Privacy policy and the otherwise sparse Blog route

## Discovery method & boundary

The XML sitemap at `/wp-sitemap.xml` identified the public sitemap groups `page-sitemap.xml`, `category-sitemap.xml`, and `ditty_news_ticker-sitemap.xml`. The live home navigation added `/blog/`. No post sitemap was advertised, and the live Blog route exposed no post listing at capture time. This archive therefore represents every public, discoverable route exposed by those sources on the capture date.
