# Non-home heroes and gallery carousel

## Scope and visitor modes
All 37 non-home archived routes. About, Programs and Contact persuade; Gallery and Clients let the artifacts lead; Blog, program outlines, privacy and archives support reading. Home and its birds component are protected and unchanged.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Give each destination an opening that demonstrates its purpose, not the same photo-right/title-left shell. Gallery foregrounds real sessions; Programs lets visitors choose a theme; Clients foregrounds the published logos; individual programs inherit topic-specific visual families.

OWN-WORLD: Keep HYtalent's teal, sage, lime and warm paper; existing Manrope/DM Sans/Playfair typography, SVG identity, VCCI navbar and real source media. Use lightweight native React/CSS/Motion adaptations of publicly researched 21st patterns, without WebGL or reinstating GSAP/Lenis.

STORY: Visitors identify the page, recognize the relevant learning topic and reach a useful real destination immediately. No invented durations, testimonials, client relationships or blog articles.

FIRST VIEWPORT: About uses a portrait-led editorial composition; Programs a four-theme expanding colour panel; Gallery a photographic mosaic; Clients a logo constellation; Blog a typographic reading index; Contact a pointer-lit direct-contact panel. Program themes vary photo proportion, reading order and surface colour. All actions remain visible and responsive.

FORM: Code-led local extensions within the established visual system, selected from the user's requested 21st collection research. No identity replacement or free concept tournament.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Constraints
Preserve every source outline, route, gallery image and contact detail. Keep HomeHero.jsx and murmuration-wordmark.tsx unchanged. Privacy and archive heroes remain static. Gallery carousel has no autoplay; support keyboard, swipe, thumbnails, lightbox, reduced motion and original-resolution image delivery.
